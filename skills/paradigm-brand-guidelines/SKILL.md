## 2. Positioning & voice

**Positioning:** PSS is a **strategic GovCon growth partner — not a consultant or vendor.** It helps
owner-operated construction and civil-infrastructure firms scale from ~$2–5M in commercial revenue to
$20–30M through federal contracting. Engagement model: monthly retainer + commission on government
invoices + optional milestone-based equity.

**Anchor every message to the three pain drivers** PSS solves — *unpredictable revenue, missed
opportunity, operational overwhelm* — and the **five-pillar process**: **Offer, Buyer, Relationship,
Proposal, Partner.**

**Voice & tone**
- **Partner, not pitchman.** Confident, direct, senior. Speaks as a strategic peer to owners,
  presidents, and founders — the only altitude where a partnership decision is made.
- **Customer-first** (consistent with the APMP doctrine): name the client and their goals before PSS;
  lead with the benefit, then the mechanism; quantify outcomes ($ and %), never vague superlatives.
- **Plain English**, not government jargon — unless quoting FAR/DFARS or a solicitation.
- **Evidence over adjectives.** Back claims with proof points; avoid hype words ("world-class,"
  "cutting-edge," "unique ability") unless immediately substantiated.
- **Never** self-sexualize the offer with false urgency or pressure; PSS earns trust, it doesn't
  corner. Relationship-first, transaction-second.

## 3. Visual system

### Colors  `⚙ CONFIRM — replace with PSS official hex`
Proposed professional GovCon palette (trust, stability, competence). Swap for the real values.

**Core**
- Ink / primary text & dark backgrounds: `#0F1B2D`  `⚙ CONFIRM`
- Paper / light background: `#F7F7F4`  `⚙ CONFIRM`
- Slate / secondary text: `#4A5568`  `⚙ CONFIRM`
- Mist / subtle fills & rules: `#E4E7EC`  `⚙ CONFIRM`

**Accent**
- Primary accent (CTAs, key stats, links): `#1F6FEB`  `⚙ CONFIRM`
- Secondary accent (success/growth, positive metrics): `#2E7D5B`  `⚙ CONFIRM`
- Signal (sparingly — warnings, deadlines): `#C05621`  `⚙ CONFIRM`

**Usage rules**
- One primary accent per view; don't rainbow. Reserve Signal for genuine urgency (deadlines, risk).
- Text on `Ink` uses `Paper`; text on `Paper` uses `Ink`. Maintain WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1 body).
- Non-text shapes/dividers use accents or `Mist`; never pure black (`#000`) — use `Ink`.

### Typography  `⚙ CONFIRM — replace with PSS official fonts`
- **Headings:** geometric/grotesque sans — proposed **Poppins** (fallback Arial). `⚙ CONFIRM`
- **Body:** readable humanist sans or serif — proposed **Inter** (fallback Helvetica/Arial), or a
  serif (e.g. Georgia) for formal proposal volumes. `⚙ CONFIRM`
- **Scale:** H1 28–32pt, H2 20–24pt, H3 16–18pt, body 10.5–12pt, caption 8–9pt.
- Headings ≥24pt get the heading face; everything else the body face. Fall back gracefully when fonts
  aren't installed in the render environment.

### Logo  `⚙ CONFIRM — supply asset files + rules`
- Provide: primary logo (color on light), reverse (light on dark), and a mark-only variant.
- Clear space = height of the logo's dominant letter/mark on all sides. Minimum width ~120px digital /
  1in print. Never stretch, recolor outside the palette, add shadows, or place on low-contrast photos.

## 4. Application by artifact

- **Proposals / capability statements:** verified identity block in the footer of every page; active,
  benefit-led headings; accent used for key stats and section rules; body face for narrative, heading
  face for headings; PDF-locked. (Content rules live in the APMP skill; this skill governs look +
  identity.)
- **Gamma decks:** heading face for titles, one primary accent, benefit-led slide titles, one idea per
  slide; contact/CTA slide uses the verified block + Calendly link.
- **One-pagers:** Ink/Paper base, single accent for the CTA and the headline metric; footer identity
  block.
- **Outreach emails:** plain, senior tone; signature = Jalen Gross + verified block; CTA points to
  Calendly. No wrong-entity details (see guardrail).
- **Client-portal / UI copy:** Slate for secondary text, primary accent for actions; consistent with
  the CRM's React/Vite frontend tokens.
- **Miro boards:** accent-coded columns; keep to the core palette so exports read as PSS.

## 5. Do / Don't

**Do:** name the client first; quantify outcomes; use one accent per view; keep the identity block
exact; substantiate every claim; keep contrast accessible.

**Don't:** ship a wrong-entity detail; use hype without proof; rainbow the palette; use pure black;
stretch/recolor the logo; let a page-builder default override these tokens.
